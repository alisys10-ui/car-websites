Islamic Car Finance USA — guided chat pilot

This is a guided inquiry chat, NOT a connected generative-AI agent.
It collects vehicle, budget, city/state, timing, name and phone, then requests consent.
It submits to Netlify Forms only on islamic-car-finance-usa.netlify.app.
Preview domains do not transmit customer data.

ACTIVATION REQUIRED
1. In the existing Netlify project enable Forms detection.
2. Deploy this folder to the EXISTING Islamic Car Finance USA project.
3. Confirm form ali-chat-leads appears in Forms.
4. Forms > Submission notifications > Add notification > Email.
   Recipient: Alisys10@gmail.com
   Form: ali-chat-leads
5. Submit a clearly labeled test inquiry from the live website.
6. Verify it appears under Forms AND arrives in the recipient inbox.
Do not consider email delivery active until both are verified.

No live deployment or email test has been performed in this package.
The existing marketing content is preserved; no inventory or financing claims
are generated by the chat. No sensitive credit information is requested.
Documentation: https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/setup/
Notifications: https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/notifications/

Calculator added: exactly five editable fields: vehicle price, annual interest rate, months, tax (default 10%), fees (default $775). Tax and fees are financed. No down payment or trade-in. APR default 0%, months default 60; illustrative only.
