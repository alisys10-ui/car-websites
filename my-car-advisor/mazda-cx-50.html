Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CX-50 and CX-50 Hybrid?

The gas CX-50 uses Mazda 2.5L naturally aspirated or turbo engines with a six-speed automatic. The CX-50 Hybrid uses a Toyota Hybrid System-based 2.5L gas-electric powertrain with e-CVT and e-AWD.

Does the gas CX-50 have a CVT?

No. Gas CX-50 models use a six-speed automatic transmission.
