What to know before you buy

  • Rear-wheel drive and driver-focused handling
  • Sport and Club are manual only
  • Grand Touring offers an optional automatic transmission

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an automatic Miata?

Yes. The 2026 Grand Touring offers an available six-speed automatic. Sport and Club are manual-only.

Is the MX-5 Miata AWD?

No. It is rear-wheel drive.
