The Mazda CX-50 Hybrid uses Toyota hybrid technology

Yes — Toyota Hybrid System (THS) technology powers the CX-50 Hybrid. Mazda states the vehicle's hybrid platform is designed around Toyota's THS architecture: a 2.5-liter gasoline engine, three electric motors, a hybrid battery, an e-CVT and electronic AWD. Mazda engineers calibrate the response to match Mazda's own driving dynamics. The result combines established Toyota hybrid technology with Mazda CX-50 styling and driving character.

Important distinction: This does not mean the entire vehicle is manufactured by Toyota, or that every part is made by Toyota. It is a Mazda vehicle using Toyota Hybrid System-based powertrain technology.

Frequently asked questions

Does the CX-50 Hybrid use Toyota hybrid technology?

Yes. Mazda states that the CX-50 Hybrid platform is designed around the Toyota Hybrid System (THS). Mazda calibrates the hybrid system for its own driving dynamics. This is a Mazda vehicle—not a Toyota-branded vehicle.

Is the hybrid engine made by Toyota?

Mazda describes the powertrain as Toyota Hybrid System-based. That is more precise than claiming the entire vehicle or every individual component was manufactured by Toyota.

Do I plug in the CX-50 Hybrid?

No. It is a conventional hybrid, not a plug-in hybrid. It charges its hybrid battery while driving and through regenerative braking.

Is the transmission a regular automatic?

It uses an e-CVT hybrid transmission, different from the six-speed automatic in the gasoline CX-50.
