Is a used CX-5 right for you?

A used Mazda CX-5 is worth comparing if you need a two-row SUV. Compare the CX-50 for cabin layout and cargo access. Check the model year and engine: equipment and powertrain choices differ across generations. Confirm year, trim, mileage and service records before comparing asking prices.

How to compare prices

There is no single fair price for every model year. Compare vehicles with similar mileage, trim, condition, accident history and warranty coverage. Request a written out-the-door quote including taxes and itemized fees. A lower advertised price may not mean a lower total cost.

Before the test drive

Ask for the VIN and vehicle-history report, maintenance records and an independent pre-purchase inspection. Check for open recalls at NHTSA.gov. Confirm tire and brake condition, the number of keys and whether any warranty is transferable.
