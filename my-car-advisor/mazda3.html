What to know before you buy

  • Mazda3 Sedan uses a 6-speed automatic
  • Mazda3 Hatchback 2.5 S Premium offers a 6-speed manual
  • AWD is available on select trims; not every Mazda3 has AWD

Frequently asked questions

Is there a manual Mazda3?

Yes. For 2026, the manual transmission is offered on the Mazda3 Hatchback 2.5 S Premium trim. Mazda3 Sedan models use automatic transmissions.

Does every Mazda3 have AWD?

No. Several trims are FWD, while the Carbon Edition and Turbo Premium Plus include AWD.
