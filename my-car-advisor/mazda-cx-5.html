Frequently asked questions

Does the 2026 CX-5 have a turbo or hybrid?

The redesigned 2026 launch model uses a 187-hp naturally aspirated 2.5L engine. Mazda has discussed a hybrid powertrain for a future model year.

Is AWD standard?

Yes. i-ACTIV AWD is standard on the 2026 CX-5.
